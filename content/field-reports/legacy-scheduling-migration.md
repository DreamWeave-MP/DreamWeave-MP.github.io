Gary had maintained the scheduling system for twenty-three years.

He had also watched five modernization programs fail.

> "They come in, tell us everything is obsolete, spend eighteen months rebuilding half of it, and then discover they did not understand why the original system worked the way it did."

His first impression of the jok3rs was unfavorable.

> "Children."
>
> "You thought they were inexperienced?"
>
> "No. I mean children. One of them was reviewing our Oracle integration. That code predates him."

On the second day, a reporting query that had historically required eleven minutes completed in forty milliseconds.

Gary did not interpret this as progress.

> "I want to know what he broke."

At 14:17, Facilities received a second report of purple smoke near the primary server room. No fire was identified. The client requested that the documentary crew leave the facility while the migration team investigated.

Recording resumed the following morning.

The DreamWeave vehicle was no longer onsite.

The replacement system was operational.

Total service interruption measured four minutes and seventeen seconds.

### Exit interview

> **Interviewer:** Do you understand how DreamWeave completed the migration?
>
> **Gary:** No.
>
> **Interviewer:** Would you recommend DreamWeave to another company?
>
> **Gary:** Yes.
>
> **Interviewer:** Would you hire them again?

Gary considered the question for several seconds.

> "I don't think we'd survive it."
