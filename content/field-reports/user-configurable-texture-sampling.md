The request was simple enough to fit in a sentence: expose nearest-neighbor texture filtering in the launcher.

The renderer already supported it.

The configuration system already supported it.

The proposed user interface was a dropdown.

By the time DreamWeave was contacted, the discussion had accumulated several months of review history, multiple competing interpretations of what the option represented, and an essay concerning digital signal reconstruction.

The client engineering lead summarized the situation during intake.

> "We are not blocked on implementation. We are blocked on what implementation means."

The first jok3r read the issue from the beginning. The second opened the renderer defaults. The third inspected the launcher code.

After several minutes they looked at one another.

All three closed their laptops.

The assistant was notified that founder involvement might be required.

No founder involvement was subsequently confirmed.

The option appeared in the next build.

The relevant change added one selectable value.

No consensus was recorded regarding what it meant philosophically.
